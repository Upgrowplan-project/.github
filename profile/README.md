# Upgrowplan

**AI-powered business planning, live market research, and deterministic financial modeling.**

[Website](https://upgrowplan.com) • [Status: Free Open Beta](https://upgrowplan.com)

---

### Overview

Upgrowplan automates business plan writing, local market research, and financial modeling for founders, analysts, and consultants. The platform generates investor- and bank-ready documents aligned with **UNIDO** and **EBRD** standards.

### Architecture & Methodology

Standard LLMs invent market statistics and make basic arithmetic errors. Upgrowplan separates data retrieval, calculation, and text generation:

* **Live Market Retrieval (RAG):** Search agents collect current pricing, rental rates, tax rules, and competitor benchmarks from 50+ open sources before generating text.
* **Deterministic Financial Modeling:** Revenue projections, unit economics, break-even points, and cash flows are calculated in **Python**, not guessed by a language model.
* **Skeptic Agent Audit:** Every section is cross-checked against niche benchmarks to flag unrealistic growth assumptions and hidden operational costs.
* **Synthetic Focus Groups:** Test value propositions and pricing against AI buyer personas tailored to a specific city and demographic before spending on acquisition.
